# M3 · Human Cognition & AI Oversight

**AI Module 3** · Weeks 14–15 · 8 hrs

**Builds on:** M2 — how AI systems work and where they fail

The human side of AI-native engineering: how our own thinking biases us, when to trust AI output, and how to design oversight. Learners apply a concrete Judgment Framework and study real oversight failures, then design human-in-the-loop checkpoints for their own domain.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners can:

- Recognise cognitive biases that trip up AI users
- Apply the three-question Judgment Framework to any AI output
- Define acceptable-error thresholds for a use case
- Design human-in-the-loop checkpoints from real failure case studies

## Scope

**In scope:** Cognitive biases and their AI impact; the Judgment Framework; oversight design from case studies

**Out of scope:** Formal cognitive-science theory; regulatory legal drafting
