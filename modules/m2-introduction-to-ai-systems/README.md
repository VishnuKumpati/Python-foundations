# M2 · Introduction to AI Systems

**AI Module 2** · Weeks 10–13 · 16 hrs

**Builds on:** M1 — computational thinking and specification writing

The core of AI fundamentals, built up progressively over four weeks: first what AI is and isn't, then the 2026 AI stack, then how LLMs actually behave, and finally ethics, safety, and governance. Learners finish able to describe modern AI systems accurately, evaluate tools, and reason about risk and regulation — without building infrastructure.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners can:

- Explain how LLMs work and where they sit in the 2026 AI stack
- Account for non-determinism using temperature and sampling
- Evaluate and compare AI tools with a weighted rubric
- Identify risks and map a system to the relevant governance framework

## Scope

**In scope:** Conceptual understanding of modern AI; tool evaluation; ethics, safety, and governance literacy

**Out of scope:** Model training or deployment; vector-database internals; building RAG or agents (concept + demo only; built in later semesters)
